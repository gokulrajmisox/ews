Welcome to SilentWindow

Traditional early-warning scores and naive ML produce severe alarm fatigue by alerting on transient spikes and noisy artifacts. SilentWindow implements a 3-layer architecture: Trust-Aware Signal Processing downweights sensor noise, Personal Baseline captures patient-specific physiological changes, and a Sequential Evidence Accumulator ensures high-confidence alerts are only triggered when deterioration is reliable and persistent.

Architectural Ablation Study

Small-cohort ablation on identical test patients; results are descriptive and do not show that every layer helps.

System Architecture Sensitivity PPV False Alarms Alerts / Patient-Day Median Lead Time Total Alerts

Trust Layer: null result here

Removing it produced identical cohort metrics to the full system. This experiment does not establish a benefit on this test set.

Personal Baseline: mixed result

Removing it kept sensitivity unchanged but reduced false-alert events from 51 to 11 and raised PPV from 15% to 75% in this cohort.

Evidence Accumulator: operating-point effect

Removing it changed sensitivity from 13.6% to 4.5%. This supports an effect in this run, not a clinical safety claim.

Noise Stress Lab & Resilience Testing

Synthetic missingness, motion spikes, and timing jitter on a small 30-patient run; descriptive stress testing, not proof of real-world immunity.
