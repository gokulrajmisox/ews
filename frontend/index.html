Welcome to SilentWindow

Traditional early-warning scores and naive ML produce severe alarm fatigue by alerting on transient spikes and noisy artifacts. SilentWindow implements a 3-layer architecture: Trust-Aware Signal Processing downweights sensor noise, Personal Baseline captures patient-specific physiological changes, and a Sequential Evidence Accumulator ensures high-confidence alerts are only triggered when deterioration is reliable and persistent.

Impact of Trust Layer

Prevents sensor artifacts (extreme jumps and loose-lead disconnects) from prematurely inflating deterioration risk or corrupting personal baselines.

Impact of Personal Baseline

Normalizes each patient to their admission physiological state, allowing detection of relative deterioration in patients who deviate from their own normal.

Impact of Evidence Accumulator

The primary filter against alert fatigue. Demands sequential persistence across multiple time steps before escalating to a high-confidence alert, avoiding single-spike false alarms.
